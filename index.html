JPMAPCB · iPhone · iOS 16+

A private portfolio journal for thoughtful investors.

JPMAPCB is your on-device investment companion — track positions, capture transactions, visualise allocation, and reflect on portfolio decisions without giving your data to a cloud platform.

Contact Support Explore Features

What JPMAPCB does

Built natively in SwiftUI for iOS 16 and later. Engineered as a portfolio journal rather than a trading terminal.

⌁

Dashboard

Net worth, total return, and day change at a glance with an interactive crosshair performance curve.

◐

Allocation

Tappable donut chart for sector allocation plus a top movers list and a monthly cash flow view.

◇

Markets

30+ reference assets across stocks, ETFs, crypto, bonds, forex, and commodities with sparklines and a heatmap.

▤

Holdings

Auto-aggregated holdings with quantity, average cost, gain, dividend income, and full transaction history.

✎

Transactions

Buy, sell, dividend, split, deposit, withdrawal, and fee events with photo attachments and notes.

∑

Tools

Currency converter, DCA simulator, scenario analyzer, rebalancer, and sector heatmap in one place.

❡

Insights

Eight long-form articles on portfolio construction, drawdowns, DCA, sector allocation, journaling, and more.

⚿

Local & Private

No account, no login, no broker connection. All data is stored on your device as JSON.

Privacy & data

JPMAPCB does not collect personal information. There is no account, no login, no telemetry, and no third-party analytics SDK. All holdings, transactions, notes, and attached photos are stored locally on your device. The app does not connect to any broker, exchange, custodian, or wallet.
