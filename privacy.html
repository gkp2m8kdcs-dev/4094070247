Privacy Policy

Effective: October 15, 2026 · App: JPMAPCB

Short version: JPMAPCB does not collect personal information. There is no account, no login, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. All your data stays on your device.

1. Overview

JPMAPCB ("the App") is designed from the ground up as a local-first portfolio companion. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, in any form, when you use it on your iPhone.

2. Information the App does not collect

  • No account, login, email, or password is required to use the App.
  • No name, address, phone number, government identifier, or financial account number is collected.
  • No contacts, calendar, location, microphone, or health data is accessed by the App.
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA) are accessed or transmitted.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs are bundled in the App.

3. Information stored on your device

The following data is created by you inside the App and stored locally on your iPhone only:

  • Holdings, transactions, notes, tags, and category labels you enter.
  • Photos and images you attach to transactions (downscaled and stored in the App's local sandbox).
  • Portfolio snapshots used to render the performance curve and dashboard.
  • App settings, theme preferences, and display options.

This data is stored as JSON documents inside the App's private container. It is never uploaded to a server, shared with a third party, or made available to other apps on your device.

4. Photos and camera

If you choose to attach a photo to a transaction, the App uses Apple's standard photo picker (PHPickerViewController) and, only when you tap the camera button, requests permission to access the camera. Photos are downscaled before being stored and remain inside the App's private container. You can revoke camera or photo library access at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Market data

The App ships with bundled reference market data for educational purposes. The App does not connect to a live market data feed and does not transmit quotes, holdings, or balances to any remote server.

6. Network usage

The App does not transmit your data to remote servers in normal operation. If you choose to use the "Send Feedback" or "Contact Support" action, only the content you compose (such as an email body) is handled by Apple's standard Mail composer; no automatic telemetry is sent.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children as defined under applicable law (including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

8. Data deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time by either:

  • Using the in-app "Reset Data" option under Settings.
  • Deleting the App from your iPhone. This removes all data inside the App's private container.

9. Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the new version will be posted at this same URL and the effective date above will be revised. Material changes will be highlighted inside the App on the next launch.
