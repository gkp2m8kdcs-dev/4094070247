Frequently asked questions

How do I back up my data?

JPMAPCB stores all data locally on your iPhone. We recommend enabling iCloud or iTunes/Finder backups for your device to keep a copy of your portfolio data along with the rest of your device backups. Inside Settings → Reset Data you can also export your portfolio as a snapshot file.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings inside JPMAPCB and choose "Reset Data", or simply delete the app from your iPhone. Both options remove every holding, transaction, note, and attached photo stored by the App.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. JPMAPCB works fully offline. The Markets tab shows bundled reference data for educational purposes. No live data is fetched and no usage telemetry is transmitted.

Can I import transactions from my brokerage?

No. JPMAPCB is intentionally a journal, not a brokerage integration. It does not connect to any broker, exchange, custodian, or wallet. You record transactions manually so you can attach notes, tags, and rationale alongside each entry.

Why are some asset prices not real-time?

JPMAPCB ships bundled reference data for educational context. The numbers shown are intended for illustration, not for trading decisions. They may be delayed or out of date.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

JPMAPCB is built natively in SwiftUI for iPhone on iOS 14 and later. It supports portrait orientation, ships with a dark theme tuned for legibility, and uses a custom drawn chart system.

How do attached photos work?

When you attach a photo to a transaction, the App uses Apple's standard photo library picker or the camera (only if you tap the camera button). Images are downscaled to keep the App lightweight and stored inside the App's private sandbox. You can revoke photo or camera access at any time in iOS Settings.

Reporting an issue

When contacting support, please include:

  • iOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • iPhone model.
  • JPMAPCB app version (visible inside the App's Settings).
  • A clear description of the issue, with steps to reproduce if possible.
